TEAM PROJECT

CampusMobi

About Our Project

CampusMobi is a web-based campus assistant designed to help students and staff easily access different university services. The system will bring important campus services together in one convenient platform.

Project Objectives

The main objective of CampusMobi is to make campus services easier and more convenient for students and staff.

  • Provide a digital student and staff card.
  • Help users navigate around campus.
  • Provide a virtual wallet.
  • Provide access to campus facilities.
  • Store and manage student information.

Main Features

  1. Digital Student Card
  2. Campus Map and Navigation
  3. Virtual Wallet
  4. Gym and Library Access
  5. Printing and Cafeteria Services
